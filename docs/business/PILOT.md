learn what is actually missing, and to let *that* decide the roadmap.

## Success, fixed in advance (change nothing after the start date)

1. Wola's opening total outstanding equals the customer's own figure to the shilling.
2. For the cycle, expected deductions per employee equal what payroll deducted, or
   every difference has a recorded cause.
3. Every application submitted during the pilot completes (approved or rejected with
   a reason) within ______ working days (agree the number).
4. Zero days where staff could not sign in during working hours.
5. Finance says, in their own words, whether they would stop using the spreadsheet.
