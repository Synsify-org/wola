**Parties.** [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME] (the **Controller**) and [YOUR LEGAL ENTITY] (the
**Processor**, operating the Wola service).

1. **Subject and duration.** The Processor processes personal data on the
   Controller's behalf to provide the Wola staff-loan service, for the term of the
   services agreement.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Recording loan applications, approvals, disbursements and
   repayments; calculating schedules and balances; reporting to the Controller's
   authorised staff.
3. **Categories of data subjects and data.** Employees of the Controller. Data: name,
   employee number, department, job title, salary (gross and net), employment
   status, loan applications and balances, login email, audit records of actions.
   **No special-category data is intended.** [Confirm with counsel.]
4. **Controller's instructions.** The Processor processes only on documented
   instructions of the Controller (this agreement and the Controller's use of the
   service) and tells the Controller if an instruction appears unlawful.
5. **Confidentiality.** People authorised by the Processor are bound by
   confidentiality. Routine access to Controller data by the Processor's staff is
   not permitted; support access happens only on request and is logged.
6. **Security.** Measures include: tenant isolation at the database level
   (row-level security) tested on every change; encrypted connections; salted
   password hashing; role-based access; optional two-step sign-in; an append-only
   audit log; daily backups with a rehearsed restore. [List hosting region(s):
   currently [DATABASE REGION], [APP REGION].]
7. **Sub-processors.** The Controller authorises: [HOSTING PROVIDER], [DATABASE
   PROVIDER], [EMAIL PROVIDER]. The Processor will give [30] days' notice of changes.
8. **International transfers.** Data is hosted in [REGION]. [Counsel: does hosting
   outside Uganda require consent or registration?]
9. **Data-subject rights.** The Processor assists the Controller, within [10]
   working days, with access, correction and deletion requests.
10. **Breach notification.** The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay,
    and within [48] hours of becoming aware, with what is known and what is being done.
11. **Return and deletion.** On termination the Processor exports the Controller's
    data in CSV within [14] days of a written request and deletes it within [30]
    days, confirming in writing. Backups expire within [35] days.
12. **Audit.** The Controller may, on [30] days' notice and not more than once a
    year, review the Processor's security documentation and the restore-drill
    records.
13. **Liability and governing law.** [Counsel.]
