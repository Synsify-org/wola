# What prospects actually said

Phase 0, first item. **Only you can fill this in**: I have no record of the
conversations. Write what they said, in their words, then tag it. The tag decides
what you do next:

- **Real gap**: the product genuinely can't do it. Candidate for the roadmap.
- **Demo-visibility gap**: it can, but they couldn't see it (a missing screen, hidden
  setting, bad demo data). Fix the demo, not the product.
- **Out of scope**: not what Wola is for. Say so politely and move on.

Done when there are at least 5 rows, each tagged, with who said it and whether it
was a **dealbreaker** (they would not buy without it).
