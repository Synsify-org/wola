## 1. What Wola is, and is not

Wola is the software that runs your staff-loan programme: applications, approvals,
payroll deductions, repayments, and the reports finance, HR and auditors need.

- **Not a lender.** The money lent is yours, and so is the interest it earns. Wola never
  holds funds, never charges staff, and never takes a share of the interest.
- **Not a new department.** It replaces the spreadsheets, emails and manual payroll
  follow-ups you already spend time on.

## 4. Your own costs (not paid to Synsify)

So the full picture is clear:

- **The money you lend.** It comes back through payroll with the interest you set.
  The CFO's interest report shows expected and collected interest by month.
- **Staff time on approvals.** Each approver sees only their own step, usually a few
  minutes per application.
- **Legal review once.** Your loan agreement and staff deduction consent.

## 5. What it saves (for the CEO's discussion)

Without inventing figures, the places Wola saves money are:

- **Finance time every month:** deduction files and reconciliation are generated, not
  typed; missed and short deductions are listed automatically.
- **Balances lost when staff leave:** an exit opens a payoff request for every running
  loan, so nothing is forgotten.
- **Audit:** every approval, payment and change is recorded and reportable.
- **A staff benefit:** fair, fast access to credit, which supports retention.

Suggested way to size it together: hours finance spends on staff loans per month ×
cost per hour, plus any balance written off in the last two years when someone left.

## 6. Why not a share of the interest?

Taking a cut of the interest would make it look as if Synsify profits from your staff's
borrowing. A fixed subscription keeps our interest aligned with yours: a programme that
runs well, with nothing hidden in the loans themselves.
